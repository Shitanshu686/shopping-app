Privacy Policy

Learn how ShopEase handles information related to your account, orders and shopping experience.

🔐 Information We Collect

ShopEase may collect information required to provide shopping and account-related services.

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account information
  • Order and transaction details
  • Product and cart-related information

🛒 How Information Is Used

Collected information may be used to provide and improve ShopEase services.

  • Manage user accounts
  • Process orders
  • Manage cart and wishlist information
  • Process payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve the shopping experience

💳 Payment Information

Payments are processed through the integrated payment service. ShopEase does not directly store sensitive payment credentials such as card numbers.

🔒 Data Security

ShopEase uses appropriate application security mechanisms to help protect account and application data from unauthorized access.

🍪 Cookies & Local Storage

ShopEase may use browser storage technologies such as LocalStorage to maintain application-related data and improve the user experience.

👤 User Responsibility

Users should keep their account credentials secure and avoid sharing passwords or authentication information with others.

Have a Privacy Question?

Contact ShopEase support if you have questions about privacy or your account information.
