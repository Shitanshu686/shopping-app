Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about shopping, orders, payments, accounts and returns on ShopEase.

🛍️ Shopping

How can I search for a product?

You can use the search bar to find products by name. ShopEase also provides categories, filters and sorting options to help you find suitable products.

Can I filter products?

Yes. Products can be filtered using category, brand, price range and minimum rating. Multiple filters can also be applied together.

Can I sort products?

Yes. Products can be sorted by price, rating and name.

👤 Account

How do I create a ShopEase account?

Open the Signup page and provide the required account information to create your ShopEase account.

Is my password stored securely?

ShopEase uses BCrypt password hashing on the backend instead of storing user passwords as plain text.

Can I change my password?

Yes. Logged-in users can change their password through the Change Password functionality.

🛒 Cart & Wishlist

Can I add products to my cart?

Yes. Products can be added to the shopping cart and quantities can be updated before checkout.

Is my cart persistent?

Yes. ShopEase provides persistent cart functionality for authenticated users.

Can I save products for later?

Yes. You can use the wishlist to save products that you may want to purchase later.

📦 Orders

How can I place an order?

Add products to your cart, proceed to checkout, provide the shipping information and complete the available payment process.

Where can I see my orders?

Logged-in users can view their previous orders through the My Orders section.

Can I see my order status?

Yes. ShopEase provides order status management so users can view the current status of their orders.

💳 Payments

Does ShopEase support online payment?

Yes. ShopEase integrates Razorpay for online payment processing.

Is payment verification implemented?

Yes. Payment signatures are verified on the backend before the payment is treated as successful.

What happens if payment fails?

Failed payments are handled without treating the order as successfully paid, and the cart can remain available for another payment attempt.

⭐ Product Ratings

Who can rate a product?

Users who have purchased a product can submit a rating after their order reaches DELIVERED status.

Can I update my rating?

Yes. Users can update their existing product rating.
